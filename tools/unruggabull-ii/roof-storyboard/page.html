The calls in this version

  • A real fight first. Three rounds, one spool each. The spools are its health bar; the odds are not. Breaking a spool never moves the odds, which is the joke until the beam finally does.
  • A lead-in. You don't walk straight into a fight: the elevator, a walk across the roof in the dark, then the lightning shows you what's at the end.
  • Souls fire from the quiet on. Before that, freed souls go to the loom.
  • A hit doesn't break the beam. Hearts still drop, so you can still lose in the crescendo, but it never stalls.
  • Continues start the round you died in (changed from the start of the fight, now that the fight is about two minutes). Losing in the crescendo puts you back at the quiet.
  • His line at the fusion: "LET'S DISCUSS THOSE ODDS."
  • Side-view cut-ins on three beats of the crescendo: the first soul, the beam erupting, the threads snapping. Each about a second, only when there's nothing to dodge, always cutting back behind him to play.
  • The beam is as wide as he is at the gun, lights the roof gold and bursts at the eye.
