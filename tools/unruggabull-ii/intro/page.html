Unruggabull II: the intro

The six stills as the game will play them: narration in blips, then each still waits for a tap (a blinking arrow shows when), Skip to end. Tap a gag to play it instead (the loom in 2 and 3, a soul in 5). Turn your phone sideways for full screen.

▸Tap

About 30 seconds.

  • Stills 5 and 6 are your new pictures: the burning tower, and Unruggabull opening the stairs door. When still 6's line ends, the doorway's light swells.
  • The Rugfather is your strip at its own pixel size: the burning heap (1), his front (2 and 4), the reaching frame (3). Everywhere he breathes, his fringe stirs, his fire flickers, his eyes glow and blink, and embers drift off him.
  • Pacing: nothing moves on by itself. Each line types out, then the still waits, moving, until you tap.
  • Not in yet: the title card and the hand-off into the stairwell. In the game the last tap goes straight to play.
