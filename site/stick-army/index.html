Stick Army

A turret, a trampoline and a notebook full of trouble.

  • Aim and hold to fire. Short bursts keep the gun from overheating.
  • Pop a chute over the trampoline to bounce him into your squad.
  • Pop it right under the plane and he rips through. Anywhere else he goes splat, squashing whoever he lands on.
  • Protect your recruits. Fallen enemies drop dog tags to spend on supplies between waves.

Mouse or touch. On a keyboard, arrows aim, space fires, B calls an air strike, C calls fighter cover, P pauses and F toggles full screen.
