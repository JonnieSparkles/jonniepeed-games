# Unruggabull II

Built from [SPEC-008](../../specs/SPEC-008-unruggabull-ii.md). The game is at `site/unruggabull-ii/index.html`. This build is Floor 13 only, the first slice of the climb. It appears as a **demo** card on Side B, keeps `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` and has local best scores only. The original game, Unruggabull: RugCo Alley, stays in its own repo.

## Finding and promoting the demo

Open Side B on the studio page (hold the rainbow egg, or `#side-b`) and choose the Unruggabull II card. To promote it after approval, remove `data-side="b"`, `data-badge="demo"`, the `.badge` span and initial `hidden` from the card, remove the noindex tag, update this doc and the README, run the checks below, stamp last and publish by hand.

## Floor 13 rules

- **Title and story.** The title screen is RugCo Tower at sunset, souls drifting up to p(Loom)'s beacon. The first Start on a device tells the story so far over it, typed out in narrator blips: a tap shows the whole line, another moves on, Skip or Escape ends it. After that, Start goes straight to the floor and the title card has a **Watch the story** link.
- **Controls.** A D-pad on the left (the up arrow jumps) and Shoot and Slash on the right. Hold Shoot to keep firing (five shots a second) or tap for one; shots lean toward the nearest temp or carpshit in a cone ahead. Like the first game, the blaster runs on charges: 20, shown under the hearts, with one back every half second. Run dry and it powers down with an "EMPTY" pop, clicks and flashes red when you try, and only fires as charges return; a chirp says it's full again. Keys: A/D or arrows to move, W, Up or Space to jump, Shift or ' to shoot (J or Z too), Enter to slash (K or X too). Keys are read by position, so holding Shift doesn't change them. M sound, F full screen, P or Escape pause. On touch screens, Start goes full screen and the pads appear under the scene (in landscape, in the bottom corners). In portrait the scene sits right on top of big pads: the D-pad on the left with jump across its top, Shoot over Slash on the right. A finger can slide between pads, and a touch in the gap between two counts for the nearer one.
- **Slash** cuts carpshits in two, knocks paper wads, bundles and staples back where they came from (a deflect, which also gives the blaster 3 charges back and flashes the charge bar), and on a pulling runner cuts the rug. Shots go straight through paper, so slashing it back or dodging are the only answers. Temps are safe from the katana behind their desks: the blaster or their own paper gets them.
- **Unruggabull** swings his arms as he runs (with a little bob), throws them up to jump, raises the blaster beside his head to shoot (bolts and the muzzle flash come from there, with a kick), swings the katana from over his shoulder, and flinches when hit. He jumps a fifth of the hall high for 0.65 seconds, stretching on takeoff and squashing on landing, with a shadow that shrinks as he rises; at the top he clears low paper and carpshits. Landing kicks up dust. At the clear he throws the horns up.
- **Incoming paper** has a jagged edge that blinks red and orange (never a round glow, so it can't pass for a pickup) and is drawn a little bigger than paper you knocked back. The Shredder's bundles are wads of shredded paper, strips sticking out every way, that spin and shed strips behind them. Nothing marks the floor. Flying carpshits ripple as they fly.
- **Pickups** float at jump height, so you jump to grab them, and are made to stand out: a beam of light from the ceiling, a glow on the floor, a halo, an outline that flashes white, sparkles going round, and a twinkle when one turns up. A RugCo coffee gives a heart back (never past five). A Spread Shot fills the blaster and fires three bolts at a time for 10 seconds. A coffee and a Spread Shot come after the audit, a coffee after lights out; temps drop a coffee one time in five when you're hurt; the Shredder drifts one in when the fight starts and at each phase change, if you're hurt.
- **Rows** span the whole aisle, so the only way past is a jump: rolling office chairs in All Staff and a paper jam's skidding sheet in the Copy Room, every 7 to 10 seconds. The first section has none, so jumping isn't needed straight away. The first row shows a "JUMP!" prompt and the first pickup "JUMP TO GRAB IT".
- **Feel.** Kills pause the action for a few frames, blaster hits throw sparks, and souls freed in a quick streak ring a step higher each time. A streak of 10 in the hall drops a Spread Shot just ahead of you ("10 STREAK!"), once per section or event.
- **The goal.** The floor opens with "FREE 90 SOULS TO WAKE THE SHREDDER", and a meter at the top fills toward it. Every kill frees one soul; the score is souls freed. (It was 60 until lights out started paying double, which made 60 a given before the copy room.)
- **The hall** comes in three beats, each announced by a sign that drops from the ceiling (the events between them get signs too, never a banner across the hall):
  1. **Accounts Payable** (26 seconds, RugCo Tower): cubicles with temps who pop up and sometimes (60%) throw a paper wad, leading you a little if you're moving; single carpshits that chase you; file boxes from 16 seconds, to jump or step around. Posters and water coolers on the walls.
  2. **AUDIT!** (8 seconds, an alarm sting, a red sign "AUDIT! SLASH IT BACK ×2", monitors gone a slow red, and a "×2" by the souls meter): a deflect round. Every desk in view is staffed, the temps stand up, and they lob slow paper at you one at a time on a beat. Each one you knock back takes out its thrower for double souls.
  3. **All Staff** (20 seconds, Alley Redux): carpshits arrive in formations (a V, a line with one gap, a snake), one hit each, and rows of office chairs roll down the aisle. Red streamers on the walls. No new formation starts in the last 5 seconds, and the lights wait (up to 8 seconds) for the last one to clear.
  4. **Lights out** (10 seconds, bass and a heartbeat, a neon sign "LIGHTS OUT! ×2 SOULS"): only monitors, eyes, paper, pickups and the runner's edges show, with you, rows and boxes faint. Blaster bolts light up the hall around them as they fly, and the muzzle flash lights you. A snake comes down the hall, single carpshits keep coming out of the dark (one hit each), and a V arrives halfway. The temps stay down. Every soul freed in the dark counts double.
  5. **Copy Room** (the Tower tune up a key): temps behind copiers, file boxes, paper sheets, and the runner rug. It ends when you reach the goal (after at least 18 seconds) or after 60 seconds regardless.
- **The runner.** In the copy room and the boss fight, the red runner warns (its edges flash) and then pulls toward the Shredder. Standing on it drags you deeper; step off, jump, or slash to cut it. The first pull shows a prompt. Riding it all the way into the mouth costs two hearts and spits you back out.
- **The Shredder** wakes at the end of the copy room: the hall stops, its eyes light, a card names it ("THE SHREDDER, HEAD OF DOCUMENT DESTRUCTION") while its health bar ticks up to full, it says its line, and the fight starts. Health 100. Blaster hits 0.3; a deflected bundle 6; a deflected staple 2.
  - Its mouth glows red with a growl 0.45 seconds before every attack.
  - The runner pulls on a steady beat in every phase, and cutting the rug in any phase jams it (no attacks, no pulls, triple blaster damage) for 2.6 seconds, up to twice that if you ride the rug in close to its mouth before you cut ("BIG JAM!"). Paper you knock back from close to the mouth hits harder too, up to double. Ride all the way in and you still lose two hearts.
  - From phase 2, every other pull is a side spray: the floor beside the rug flashes red while it warns, then staples stream down both sides (not the rug) while it pulls. Ride the rug (and cut it when you like), or jump the staples at the side. The first shows "SIDE SPRAY! RIDE THE RUG OR JUMP THE STAPLES". No other attacks during a spray.
  - Phase 1 (above 66): an attack every 1.8 seconds, alternating a bundle (2.2 seconds to reach you) and a volley of two scraps of shredded paper 0.35 seconds apart (1.7 seconds each, worth 3 knocked back), for tap-tap deflects. No attacks while the runner pulls. Pulls of 2.4 seconds, 6 seconds apart.
  - Phase 2 (above 33): an attack every 2 seconds, cycling a staple fan (four with one gap), a quicker bundle (1.6 seconds), a paper jam sheet that skids out of its mouth across the whole floor (jump it), and a volley of three scraps. Pulls of 3.2 seconds, 5.5 apart.
  - Into phase 3 it says "ENTERING POWER SAVING MODE." and the lights go out for 7 seconds: only its eyes, its teeth, the paper and the runner's edges show, and the bolts light the way.
  - Phase 3: the rally. It braces (blaster hits do half damage unless it's jammed or reeling) and cycles the rally, a staple fan, a staple carpet (a row of staples across the whole floor: jump it) and a volley of three scraps, every 1.8 seconds. The rally's bundle is bigger, with a white-hot edge inside the red. Knock it back and the Shredder bats it back at you ("RETURN TO SENDER." the first time), quicker each time (1.7 seconds to start, 0.87 times as long each return, never under 0.8), with a "RALLY xN" counter and a rising ping. It bats back 1 to 3 of your returns, then misses: a smash for 10 + 4 per bat-back, and it reels (stunned and open to triple blaster damage) for 1.6 seconds. Miss a return and the rally ends with a heart lost. No attacks or pulls while a rally is in play, and a rally waits for a pull to end; the first serve shows "RALLY! KEEP KNOCKING IT BACK". Pulls of 3.8 seconds, 5 apart.
- **Clear.** The final hit flashes white and freezes for a moment, then 1.3 seconds run at a third of the speed while the Shredder shudders, then it blows and says "RUG NOT FOUND." Thirteen souls come out of it, Unruggabull throws the horns up, "FLOOR 13 CLEAR", then a card with souls freed, time, deflects, best streak, smashes, continues (if any) and best.
- **Rugged.** Five hearts, 1.7 seconds of safety after each hit. At zero in the hall, "Rugged. Continue?" starts the floor over. Beaten by the Shredder, Continue picks up at the fight with full hearts and the souls you had when it woke (it says "BACK FOR SECONDS?"), and Restart floor starts over. The clock keeps running across continues, and a clear with continues doesn't set the fastest clear.

Best souls and fastest clear are kept in `localStorage` (`unruggabull-ii-best`), whether the story has been seen in `unruggabull-ii-story`, and sound mute in `unruggabull-ii-muted`.

## Code entry points

- `art.js` (`UnrugArt`): palette, 3x5 font, sprites as text grids, and the bull's poses from behind, cached once.
- `audio.js` (`UnrugSound`): `init`, `play(name)`, `muted`, `toggle`, `music('theme' | 'tower' | 'staff' | 'dark' | 'copy' | 'shred' | null)`, `say(text, who)` for blip talk, `hush()` to cut a line short, and `talkTimes` so captions type out in step with it. The chip sequencer and all six tracks live here.
- `game.js`: `TUNE` holds the numbers (charges, recharge, Spread Shot time) and `BEATS` the hall's three beats with their music. `newRun`, `update(dt)` and `draw()` are the core; `updateHall` runs the beats and events, `updatePull`, `updateBoss` and `updatePhase` the runner, the Shredder and the floor's flow, and `STORY` with `startIntro` the opening. The corridor projection (`PX`, `FY`, `YH`) and the far-wall box `BACK` are at the top; `drawTower` is the title scene. Stations, decor and signs are drawn before anything that moves, so they never cut through it.

## Balance bots

The game is opted in to the shared balance bots ([01: Balance bots](../guides/01-balance-bots.md)): `tests/unruggabull-ii/balance.js` (the hookup), `bot.js` and `profiles.json`, and `balance.json` (report columns). Run `python3 tools/balance/run.py unruggabull-ii --runs 100`; add `--skills decent --ref main` for a before/after on a tuning change, which tuning PRs need from now on.

- **Stages** stand in for the report's waves: 1 Accounts Payable, 2 Audit, 3 All Staff, 4 Lights out, 5 Copy Room, 6 to 8 the Shredder's phases, 9 cleared. "How runs end" names what took the last heart (`wad`, `formation`, `chairs`, `sheet`, `box`, `rug`, `bundle`, `staple`, `rally`), or `cleared`.
- **Columns:** souls by blaster, katana, deflect, audit bonus and dark bonus; streak drops; deflects; hearts lost by cause (side-spray staples as `spray`, scraps as `scrap`, staple carpets as `carpet`); coffee and Spread Shots grabbed; times the blaster ran dry; rally returns, smashes, rug cuts and jams; and Shredder damage by blaster, deflects and smashes.
- **The bot** plays through the same inputs a person does and sees the game `reaction_ms` late. Per profile: `notice_s` (how long before something new registers), `depth_err` (how far off its sense of depth is), `deflect_try`, `fly_slash`, `dodge`, `jump_try` and `pickup` (how often it tries each move), `jump_err`, `charge_floor` (charges it keeps back), `cut_try` (how often it stays on a pulling runner and cuts it instead of stepping off), `ride` (how far toward the mouth it rides before cutting, as a share of the way) and `anticipate` (how much of its reaction delay it leads its timing by). It sees itself late too, but counts in what it has pressed since, as a person would, so it doesn't overshoot where it's going. It gets onto the rug for a side spray. It doesn't see the dark: in lights out and power saving mode it plays as if the lights were on, so those parts are harder for people than the numbers show.
- **The game's side:** outcomes come from one seeded stream and cosmetics from `Math.random`, `#seed=42` replays a run, `emit()` reports events (a no-op in play), and `looping = false` stops the frame loop. `case-08-seeds.js` and `--verify` check that drawing never changes a run.
- **Calibration** (100 seeds each): casual reaches the Copy Room (median stage 5, 76 souls, 28% reach the Shredder); decent clears 64% of its runs (100 souls); expert clears 99%. The first pass looked much harder because the bot overshot its own moves: seeing itself late, it wobbled side to side and walked into paper it had judged would miss. That fix puts the bots well ahead of the owner's last reported best (46 souls, several builds back), so the profiles need recalibrating against fresh playtests. Playtesting wins.

## Validation

Serve `site/` on port 8000 and run `python3 tests/unruggabull-ii/test.py`. The cases step the game tick by tick through a test-only bridge (injected into the response, never shipped): shooting and slashing in the hall, blaster charges, pickups, rows to jump, streaks, the three beats with their events and music, the runner, the Shredder's phases and jams, the story, the clear and rugged cards, pause and the keyboard; and (`case-09-extras.js`) lights out's double souls, streak drops, the Shredder's intro, pulls and jams in every phase, the rally waiting for a pull, power saving mode, continuing at the Shredder and the clear card's stats; and (`case-10-boss-variety.js`) the Shredder's attack patterns, volleys, sheets and staple carpets, riding the rug in, and side sprays. Then it checks layout at desktop, phone portrait and phone landscape, including the story and full screen on Start, and real touch on the pads (sliding between arrows, holding Shoot). The studio harness covers the Side B card.

Difficulty is measured with the balance bots above; a full clear takes about two minutes. It still needs playtesting on real phones, and playtesting wins.

`python3 tools/og/make.py --game unruggabull-ii` rebuilds the share card and shelf thumbnail from the title screen.
