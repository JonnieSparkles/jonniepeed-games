# Unruggabull II

Built from [spec.md](spec.md); why things are the way they are, and what was tried and dropped, is in [history.md](history.md). The game is at `site/unruggabull-ii/index.html`. This build is Floor 13 only, the first slice of the climb. It's unlisted: no card on either shelf, only its direct link, and it keeps `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` and has local best scores only. The original game, Unruggabull: RugCo Alley, stays in its own repo.

## Finding and promoting the demo

Go to `unruggabull-ii/` directly; nothing on the studio page links to it. To list it, add a card to `site/index.html` (on Side B, a `data-side="b" data-badge="demo" hidden` card like Stick Army's; the thumbnail is `unruggabull-ii/thumb.webp`) and update the studio harness's card counts. To promote it after approval, give it a Side A card, remove the noindex tag, update this doc and the README, run the checks below, stamp last and publish by hand.

## Floor 13 rules

- **Title and story.** The title screen is RugCo Tower at sunset, souls drifting up to p(Loom)'s beacon. The first Start on a device tells the story so far over it, typed out in narrator blips: a tap shows the whole line, another moves on, Skip or Escape ends it. After that, Start goes straight to the floor and the title card has a **Watch the story** link.
- **Controls.** A thumb stick on the left and Shoot and Slash on the right. Hold Shoot to keep firing (five shots a second) or tap for one; shots lean toward the nearest temp or carpshit in a cone ahead. Like the first game, the blaster runs on charges: 20, shown under the hearts, with one back every half second. Run dry and it powers down with an "EMPTY" pop, clicks and flashes red when you try, and only fires as charges return; a chirp says it's full again. Keys: A/D or arrows to move, W, Up or Space to jump, S or Down to crouch, Shift or ' to shoot (J or Z too), Enter to slash (K or X too). Keys are read by position, so holding Shift doesn't change them. M sound, F full screen, P or Escape pause. On touch screens, Start goes full screen and the controls appear under the scene (in landscape, in the bottom corners). The stick has no frame, just a faint ring where it rests, and it floats: put your thumb down anywhere in its area and that's the centre (a hint under it shows until the first touch). Slide left or right to move, flick up to jump (once per flick; come back toward the middle to flick again), hold down to crouch. If the thumb wanders well past the ring, the centre follows it. Shoot and Slash are round arcade buttons, blue with a pixel blaster and red with a pixel katana, that sink and glow while held. The ring round Shoot is the blaster's charge (red when low, grey while the blaster is down); the ring round Slash sweeps back as it's ready again. In portrait the scene sits right on top of the stick's area and Shoot over Slash, and the title card says it's best played in landscape; in landscape Slash sits a little higher than Shoot. A finger can slide between Shoot and Slash, and a touch in the gap between them counts for the nearer one.
- **Crouch.** Held on the ground only: half height, so anything at head height passes over, but no moving or shooting (slashing still works); on touch screens Shoot greys out while you're down. Jumping from a crouch works. Standing on a pulling runner, crouching grips it: it drags you at a quarter of the speed ("GRIP!").
- **Slash** cuts carpshits in two, knocks paper wads, bundles and staples back where they came from (a deflect, which also gives the blaster 3 charges back and flashes the charge bar), and on a pulling runner cuts the rug. Shots go straight through paper, so slashing it back or dodging are the only answers. Temps are safe from the katana behind their desks: the blaster or their own paper gets them.
- **Unruggabull talks**: about seventeen short lines in a small caption at the top left, under the hearts, each once per run (the floor's start, the first deflect, moving day ("LIFT WITH YOUR LEGS."), lights out, the first Spread Shot and coffee, a streak, the copy room, an answer to the Shredder's opening line, the first jam and smash, one heart left, the clear, and "WHOA!" when he's rugged, "HEY! THAT WAS MINE." when the surge takes his blaster, and "CLEAN SWEEP." for his first formation wipe). Long lines wrap into short ones, so the caption stays at the left, clear of the signs hanging over the hall. He talks in his own blips for now; the lines in `LINES` in `game.js` are the recording script for Jonnie's voice. He never talks over the Shredder.
- **Unruggabull** swings his arms as he runs (with a little bob), throws them up to jump, raises the blaster beside his head to shoot (bolts and the muzzle flash come from there, with a kick), swings the katana from over his shoulder, and flinches when hit. He jumps a fifth of the hall high for 0.65 seconds, stretching on takeoff and squashing on landing, with a shadow that shrinks as he rises; at the top he clears low paper and carpshits. Landing kicks up dust. Hit, he staggers a step back toward you, rocking side to side (drawn only). Standing about, he breathes; after 2 seconds he glances left and right; after 5 he does something, in turn: spins the blaster round his finger, checks the katana in its sheath, scuffs a hoof. Moving, shooting, slashing, jumping or crouching resets it. At the clear he throws the horns up.
- **Incoming paper** has a jagged edge that blinks red and orange (never a round glow, so it can't pass for a pickup) and is drawn a little bigger than paper you knocked back. The Shredder's bundles are wads of shredded paper, strips sticking out every way, that spin and shed strips behind them. Nothing marks the floor. Flying carpshits ripple as they fly.
- **Pickups** float at jump height, so you jump to grab them, and are made to stand out: a beam of light from the ceiling, a glow on the floor, a halo, an outline that flashes white, sparkles going round, and a twinkle when one turns up. A RugCo coffee gives a heart back (never past five). A Spread Shot fills the blaster and fires three bolts at a time for 10 seconds. A coffee and a Spread Shot come after moving day, a coffee after lights out; temps drop a coffee one time in five when you're hurt; the Shredder drifts one in when the fight starts and at each phase change, if you're hurt.
- **Rows** span the whole aisle, so the only way past is a jump: three rolling office chairs side by side in All Staff (high backs, five-star bases, spinning casters and a swivel wobble; they tip over if one hits you) and a paper jam's sheet in the Copy Room (a wall-to-wall printed page curling up at you, its corner flapping), every 7 to 10 seconds. The first section has none, so jumping isn't needed straight away. The first row shows a "JUMP" prompt and the first pickup "JUMP TO GRAB".
- **Words on screen** are kept few. Prompts during play are one short line in a small strip at the top, never across the hall: JUMP, DUCK, JUMP TO GRAB, STEP OFF OR SLASH, SLASH THE RUG, RIDE THE RUG, SLASH IT BACK, WIN A RALLY FOR IT, each once. A deflect pops just its charges ("+3"); kills in the dark don't pop "×2" (the meter shows it).
- **Feel.** Only one "EMPTY" shows at a time, and a pop with the same words close by isn't repeated. Kills pause the action for a few frames, blaster hits throw sparks, and souls freed in a quick streak ring a step higher each time. Each freed soul floats up, then flies into the souls counter, which pulses as it lands (the count shown catches up as they land). At one heart the screen edges pulse red with a heartbeat. Now and then a desk phone rings down the hall, and a copier whirs in the copy room. A streak of 10 in the hall drops a Spread Shot just ahead of you ("10 STREAK!"), once per section or event. Wipe out every carpshit in a formation before any of it is halfway down the hall and it pays a bonus soul for each, with a "WIPED OUT +5" pop, a chime and sparks; in lights out it's a big moment instead (a big "WIPED OUT! +5", a fanfare and a beat of hit-stop).
- **The goal.** The floor opens with "FLOOR 13 / ACCOUNTING" then "FREE 90 SOULS", and a meter at the top fills toward it. Every kill frees one soul; the score is souls freed.
- **The hall** comes in three beats, each announced by a sign that drops from the ceiling (the events between them get signs too, never a banner across the hall):
  1. **Accounts Payable** (26 seconds, RugCo Tower): cubicles with temps (three looks: a red tie; glasses and a blue tie; a comb-over and a teal tie, picked by the desk) who pop up and sometimes (60%) throw a paper wad, leading you a little if you're moving (a temp about to throw flashes a "!" card first); single carpshits that chase you; file boxes from 16 seconds, to jump or step around. Posters and water coolers on the walls.
  2. **Moving day** (13 seconds, a hazard-yellow sign "MOVING DAY"): an athletic stretch with nothing to shoot. Carpshits clear out, the temps stay down and the blaster is holstered (Shoot does nothing). Facilities has left the furniture in the hall, one piece every 1.15 seconds in a set order: walls of three tall box stacks with one gap (too tall to jump; the gap swaps sides each time, so you zig-zag), rows of chairs to jump, and striped beams hung at head height to duck. Furniture never costs a heart: run into it and you bump it and stagger. Each piece you get past cleanly frees a soul; get through without a bump and it's "NOT A SCRATCH +10".
  3. **All Staff** (20 seconds, Alley Redux): carpshits arrive in formations (a V, a line with one gap that comes in at head height, so duck it or find the gap, and a snake), one hit each, and rows of office chairs roll down the aisle. Red streamers on the walls. No new formation starts in the last 5 seconds, and the lights wait (up to 8 seconds) for the last one to clear.
  4. **Lights out** (10 seconds, bass and a heartbeat, a neon sign "LIGHTS OUT ×2"): only monitors, eyes, paper, pickups and the runner's edges show, with you, rows and boxes faint. Blaster bolts light up the hall around them as they fly, and the muzzle flash lights you. A snake comes down the hall, then a formation every 2.2 seconds (a V, a high line, a snake) and single carpshits every second or so (one hit each). The temps stay down. Every soul freed in the dark counts double. Every 3 seconds a light switch glows on one wall; shoot it (move toward that wall and your shots lean to it) and the lights flash on for 1.4 seconds, freezing every carpshit in the hall while they still pay double. Far down the endless hall, two red eyes open now and then, watching.
  5. **Copy Room** (the Tower tune up a key): temps behind copiers, file boxes, paper sheets, and the runner rug. It ends when you reach the goal (after at least 18 seconds) or after 60 seconds regardless.
- **The endless hall.** Until the copy room the hall has no end: it runs on into the fog, ceiling lights and runner and all. In the copy room the Shredder appears far down it and closes in as you near the goal (souls and the room's time both count), reaching the end of the hall as it wakes.
- **The runner.** In the copy room and the boss fight, the red runner warns (its edges flash) and then pulls toward the Shredder. Standing on it drags you deeper; step off, jump, slash to cut it, or crouch to grip it. The first pull shows a prompt ("STEP OFF OR SLASH"). Riding it all the way into the mouth costs two hearts and spits you back out.
- **The Shredder** wakes at the end of the copy room: the hall stops, its eyes light, a card names it ("THE SHREDDER, HEAD OF DOCUMENT DESTRUCTION") while its health bar ticks up to full, it says its line, and the fight starts. Health 100. Blaster hits 0.3; a deflected bundle 6; a deflected staple or paper airplane 2.
  - **Paper airplanes**, in every phase's cycle: a flight of five spreads across the hall at head height (1.6 seconds to reach you), one aimed at you and the others 0.3 apart, so sidestepping lands you in the next one. Duck under them, or slash them back. The first thing at head height (a plane, a high line or a beam) shows "DUCK".
  - Its mouth glows red with a growl 0.45 seconds before every attack. It wears down as you hurt it: up to eight cracks spread across its panels, it smokes past 30% damage, and sparks fly from the cracks past 60%.
  - **It's alive.** Its body is a spring, drawn on its own canvas and scaled with hard pixels about the foot of its mouth: it breathes (faster in phase 3), rears back as its mouth glows, lunges at you as it spits, recoils from deflects and smashes (a smash also sets it shuddering), shudders while jammed, and roars forward into each new phase. Its pupils follow you across the hall, it blinks now and then, and from phase 2 (or while winding up) its brows come down. When you miss a return or it hits you, it lunges forward laughing, its eyes squeezed into arcs and its teeth chattering. Shredded strips drop from its mouth onto the rug while it chews, faster during a pull. Below 15 it flickers and jitters and says "I AM NOT... OUT OF ORDER." once.
  - **It talks back**, never more than once every 12 seconds and never over another line or a banner, each list in turn: when you miss a return ("SHREDDED.", "DENIED.", "FILED UNDER NOPE."), when its paper hits you ("PAPER CUT.", "SIGN HERE. AND HERE.", "THAT GOES ON YOUR RECORD."), and when you smash it ("MY ROLLERS!", "PC LOAD LETTER?!", "THAT VOIDS MY WARRANTY."). Not on the hit that rugs you: his "WHOA!" cuts off whatever it's saying.
  - The runner pulls on a steady beat in every phase, and cutting the rug in any phase jams it (no attacks, no pulls, triple blaster damage) for 2.6 seconds, up to twice that if you ride the rug in close to its mouth before you cut ("BIG JAM!"). Paper you knock back from close to the mouth hits harder too, up to double. Ride all the way in and you still lose two hearts.
  - From phase 2, every other pull is a side spray: the floor beside the rug flashes red while it warns, then staples stream down both sides (not the rug) while it pulls. Ride the rug (and cut it when you like), or jump the staples at the side. The first shows "RIDE THE RUG". No other attacks during a spray.
  - **Rallies**, in every phase: it serves a white-hot bundle and bats your returns back, each one quicker (1 second to start, 0.85 times as long each return, never under 0.45; your returns fly back quicker too), then misses: a smash for 6 + 2 per bat-back, and it reels for 1.6 seconds. It bats back 1 to 2 returns in phase 1, 2 to 4 in phase 2 and 3 to 5 in phase 3. Once a phase it's a **marathon** ("MARATHON!", and the first time "LET'S GO THE DISTANCE."): 6 returns in phase 1, 7 in phase 2, 8 in phase 3, finishing in a bigger smash ("MARATHON SMASH!", a white flash, a freeze and an explosion). A rally builds as it goes: a counter at the top ("RALLY x3", doubling in size from 4, flashing with each return), speed streaks round the bundle, a rising ping, harder shakes, and the music speeding up (up to 1.4 times), settling back when it ends. Miss a return and the rally ends with a heart lost. No attacks or pulls while a rally is on, and rallies wait for a pull to end. The first serve shows "SLASH IT BACK" and the first bat-back gets "RETURN TO SENDER." A slash reaches 0.19 of the hall ahead and knocks paper back for 0.2 seconds after you press it; each deflect draws a bright cut line from the blade to the paper, so one at arm's length reads as reached.
  - Phase 1 (above 66): an attack every 1.4 seconds, cycling a rally, a bundle (2.2 seconds to reach you), a volley of three scraps of shredded paper 0.24 seconds apart (1.4 seconds each, worth 3 knocked back) for tap-tap deflects, the marathon, and paper airplanes. It keeps attacking while the runner pulls (but not while it warns). Pulls of 2.4 seconds, 6 seconds apart.
  - Phase 2 (above 33): an attack every 1.5 seconds, cycling a staple fan (four with one gap), the marathon, a quicker bundle (1.6 seconds), paper airplanes, a rally, a paper jam sheet that skids out of its mouth across the whole floor (jump it), and a volley of four scraps. Pulls of 3.2 seconds, 5.5 apart.
  - Into phase 3 it says "ENTERING POWER SAVING MODE." and the lights go out for 7 seconds: only its eyes, its teeth, the paper and the runner's edges show, and the bolts light the way.
  - Phase 3: the volley match. A second after the lights go out, a power surge you can't dodge flashes blue and knocks the blaster out of his hands, spinning up the rug ("WIN A RALLY FOR IT"). The cause is shown plainly: for 0.9 seconds before every surge its eyes flicker blue and white and crackle with blue sparks (with a growl), then a jagged bolt of blue lightning runs from its eyes to the gun in his hand. It lies on the rug crackling; pulls carry it toward the mouth, and Shoot only clicks. Win a rally and the smash makes the Shredder reel and the rug roll back toward you ("REWIND!", blue edges), carrying the blaster to the front, where it flashes gold and you walk onto it to grab it. The first time you get it back, three souls leave the counter one after another (0.6 seconds apart, 1.4 seconds each, each on its own wide arc, drawn double size with a glow and a sparkle trail) and fly into the gun; the first two each land with one soft bell (D5, then F5) and add a stream, thin and white with a gold halo and tail, beside the bolt (two streams, then three, fanning slightly, never bending in). After four seconds of firing (the hold doesn't run while he isn't shooting) the streams drop off one by one, each one's soul leaving the gun and flying back to the counter, 0.6 seconds apart, without a sound. Nothing announces it (the live region only says "Blaster back."), the counter keeps its souls, and the streams do normal damage for no extra charges. It's the first glimpse of the mega stream, which fuses only on the roof ([spec](spec.md#the-mega-stream)). A rolling rug shoves anyone on it to the back of the hall and pins them there: no sidestepping. It cycles a rally, another surge (a rally instead if the blaster's already gone), the marathon, a rewind (the rug rolls back and a bundle comes at you: pinned on the rug, knock it back), paper airplanes, a rally, a staple fan, a volley of five scraps and a staple carpet, every 1.4 seconds. It braces (blaster hits do half damage unless it's jammed or reeling). No attacks or pulls while a roll is on, and rewinds wait for a pull to end. Pulls of 3.8 seconds, 5 apart.
- **Clear.** The final hit flashes white and freezes for a moment, then 1.3 seconds run at a third of the speed while the Shredder shudders, then it blows and says "RUG NOT FOUND." Thirteen souls come out of it, the souls freed on the floor (up to 40 of them) drift up past him and away, Unruggabull throws the horns up, "FLOOR 13 CLEAR", then a card with souls freed, time, deflects, best streak, formation wipes, smashes, longest rally, continues (if any) and best.
- **Rugged.** Five hearts, 1.7 seconds of safety after each hit. At zero the rug is yanked out from under him (the runner's stripes zip away): he flips up, spins in quarter turns and falls off the bottom of the screen. In the hall, "Rugged. Continue?" starts the floor over. Beaten by the Shredder, Continue picks up at the fight with full hearts and the souls you had when it woke (it says "BACK FOR SECONDS?"), and Restart floor starts over. The clock keeps running across continues, and a clear with continues doesn't set the fastest clear.

Best souls and fastest clear are kept in `localStorage` (`unruggabull-ii-best`), whether the story has been seen in `unruggabull-ii-story`, and sound mute in `unruggabull-ii-muted`.

## Code entry points

- `art.js` (`UnrugArt`): palette, 3x5 font, sprites as text grids, and the bull's poses from behind, cached once.
- `audio.js` (`UnrugSound`): `init`, `play(name)`, `muted`, `toggle`, `music('theme' | 'tower' | 'staff' | 'dark' | 'copy' | 'shred' | null)`, `tempo(rate)` (a rally speeds the music up), `play('bell', n)` for a soul joining the blaster, `say(text, who)` for blip talk, `hush()` to cut a line short, and `talkTimes` so captions type out in step with it. The chip sequencer and all six tracks live here.
- `game.js`: `TUNE` holds the numbers (charges, recharge, Spread Shot time, the souls' hint as `soulHint`) and `BEATS` the hall's three beats with their music. `newRun`, `update(dt)` and `draw()` are the core; `updateHall` runs the beats and events, `updatePull`, `updateBoss` and `updatePhase` the runner, the Shredder and the floor's flow, and `STORY` with `startIntro` the opening. The corridor projection (`PX`, `FY`, `YH`) and the far-wall box `BACK` are at the top; `drawTower` is the title scene. Stations, decor and signs are drawn before anything that moves, so they never cut through it.

## Balance bots

The game is opted in to the shared balance bots ([01: Balance bots](../../guides/01-balance-bots.md)): `tests/unruggabull-ii/balance.js` (the hookup), `bot.js` and `profiles.json`, and `balance.json` (report columns). Run `python3 tools/balance/run.py unruggabull-ii --runs 100`; add `--skills decent --ref main` for a before/after on a tuning change, which tuning PRs need from now on.

- **Stages** stand in for the report's waves: 1 Accounts Payable, 2 Moving day, 3 All Staff, 4 Lights out, 5 Copy Room, 6 to 8 the Shredder's phases, 9 cleared. "How runs end" names what took the last heart (`wad`, `formation`, `chairs`, `sheet`, `box`, `rug`, `bundle`, `staple`, `scrap`, `plane`, `rally`), or `cleared`.
- **Columns:** souls by blaster, katana, deflect, dark and wipe bonuses, and from moving day; formation wipes; streak drops; deflects; hearts lost by cause (side-spray staples as `spray`, scraps as `scrap`, staple carpets as `carpet`, paper airplanes as `plane`); coffee and Spread Shots grabbed; times the blaster ran dry; rally returns, smashes, rug cuts and jams; times disarmed, rug roll-backs and blasters grabbed back; and Shredder damage by blaster, deflects and smashes.
- **The bot** plays through the same inputs a person does and sees the game `reaction_ms` late. Per profile: `notice_s` (how long before something new registers), `depth_err` (how far off its sense of depth is), `deflect_try`, `fly_slash`, `dodge`, `jump_try` and `pickup` (how often it tries each move), `jump_err`, `charge_floor` (charges it keeps back), `cut_try` (how often it stays on a pulling runner and cuts it instead of stepping off), `ride` (how far toward the mouth it rides before cutting, as a share of the way) and `anticipate` (how much of its reaction delay it leads its timing by). It sees itself late too, but counts in what it has pressed since, as a person would, so it doesn't overshoot where it's going. It gets onto the rug for a side spray. On moving day it heads for the gap in a wall and ducks a beam (with its `jump_try` odds and `jump_err` timing). It doesn't shoot light switches on purpose, though its shots lean to one when it's near that wall. A rally it always tries to return, as a person would: it swings for the middle of the slash's window, and each return its timing is off by a fresh amount in time (about `depth_err` × 1.5 seconds), on top of the reaction delay it doesn't lead by, so faster returns are harder for it. Paper airplanes it knocks back (`deflect_try`) or ducks (`dodge`), and a high line it cuts (`fly_slash`) or ducks; it doesn't use the grip. It doesn't see the dark: in lights out and power saving mode it plays as if the lights were on, so those parts are harder for people than the numbers show.
- **The game's side:** outcomes come from one seeded stream and cosmetics from `Math.random`, `#seed=42` replays a run, `emit()` reports events (a no-op in play), and `looping = false` stops the frame loop. `case-08-seeds.js` and `--verify` check that drawing never changes a run.
- **Calibration** (100 seeds each): casual reaches the Copy Room (median stage 5, 96 souls, 47% reach the Shredder and few get past phase 1's rallies); decent clears 66% of its runs (63% over 300 seeds; 133 souls); expert clears 100%. The first pass looked much harder because the bot overshot its own moves: seeing itself late, it wobbled side to side and walked into paper it had judged would miss. That fix puts the bots well ahead of the owner's last reported best (46 souls, several builds back), so the profiles need recalibrating against fresh playtests. Playtesting wins.
