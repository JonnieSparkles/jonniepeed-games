# Unruggabull II: the ending

Status: storyboarded with the owner on his phone, Oct 14 2026 (https://claude.ai/artifact/GUAmJJt23c8HCAJLHdmp7C, version 8), and built as a first pass on Oct 15 with stand-in drawing, "knowing it'll be tweaked later". This is the plan as it was decided; the ending as it is now is in [README.md](README.md) (**The ending**, `ending.js`), and why it is this way is in [history.md](history.md).

## The direction

A mix of two of the takes he was offered, "The Wink" and "One Last Rug Pull". The souls are free and the win stands; p(Loom) isn't quite dead, and it gets away. Dry and small: the roof's fight already has its own fake-out (ALL SOULS ACCOUNTED FOR, then the souls tear loose), so this one is a joke, not a second rise.

1. **Dawn breathes.** The storm has passed. Souls drift into the sunrise. He lowers the blaster. The victory lands.
2. **The dead eye.** Close on p(Loom)'s wrecked eye: dark, cracked, apparently destroyed.
3. **The flicker.** A tiny blue light inside it, baby Loomy's eye from the intro. It blinks once.
4. **Not quite dead.** The eye flashes red. Sparks. The machinery groans.
5. **The escape.** The eye tears itself loose from the loom, snapping cables and threads, and is gone beyond the roof. How it gets away is never explained.
6. **The reaction.** Back to him. A beat of silence. "OH, COME ON."
7. **Final card.** Fade to black: "... TO BE CONTINUED... MAYBE."

Rules: the 480 × 270 look; dry, understated humour; freeing the souls stays a win. No Sky Wrecker and nothing about how the eye travels (the sequel decides). "YOU UNRUGGED MY FATHER. THANK YOU." is out.

## The shots

About 28.5 seconds (the dead eye stays dark 1.5 s longer, owner, Oct 16), every time a starting point. Hard cuts, except the fade to black into the card.

1. **Dawn breathes.** The roof's own dawn (the snap, the sunrise, the souls rising out of the wreck), held a moment longer while the HUD fades away. Nothing new on screen.
2. **The tower at sunrise.** RugCo Tower from outside, its roof pavilion dark and broken, threads hanging over the edge, souls coming up out of it into the sunrise. It answers the intro's still 5, where they went in. The view pans up the picture from the roof to open sky (210 px over 6 s, about still 5's speed, the other way); the souls are drawn over it. This shot was Claude's addition; he kept it ("i dig shot two").
3. **The dead eye.** An extreme close-up of the eye in its riveted socket; slack threads across the top, the rug at the bottom, sunrise light from the upper left. Nothing for 1.6 s; then a tiny blue light fades up in the pupil; at 2.5 s it blinks once, with the clack from tapping baby Loomy in the intro; at 3.3 s the iris floods red, the cracks glow, sparks spit from them and the frame shakes. The blue is gone.
4. **It tears loose.** Back behind him on the roof. The eye is red; he snaps the blaster up by reflex. It jerks on its rail; four cables snap in turn; at 1.3 s it rips out in a burst of thread and sparks with a white flash, and goes straight up out of the picture, faster and faster, trailing a thread. Then nothing: the empty socket, the thread swinging, and he lowers the blaster.
5. **The reaction.** The reverse angle, from the loom's end: he faces the camera the whole time (owner: "he stays facing the camera 100%"), lit by the sunrise; behind him the roof runs back to the elevator housing under a sky still night blue, a water tank at the left and a low skyline beyond the far parapet. From 0.3 to 1.7 s, far off behind him, p(Loom) falls as a tiny red speck and drops out of sight behind the skyline (owner: "correct out of sight"); he never sees it. At 1.8 s his caption types out "OH, COME ON." This replaced a single thread drifting down through the shot; the fall was slowed from 1.1 s to 1.4 s ("a TOUCH slower").
6. **Final card.** The reaction fades to black over 0.8 s. "... TO BE CONTINUED..." types out, a beat of about half a second, then "MAYBE." on its own: the beat before MAYBE. is the joke, so keep it when retiming. It holds; no sting. (It was "POSSIBLY TO BE CONTINUED." until he asked; a p(Loom)-odds variant, "PROBABILITY: 1%.", was offered and not taken.)

Calls he approved or kept: the tower shot; the blink with Loomy's clack; the blaster up by reflex and down only once it's gone; the speck; no HUD after shot 1.

## Pictures

Only shot 2 has a generated picture. Shots 3 and 5 stay drawn in code (owner, Oct 14), with their polish in the roof's art pass (Part B): the eye close-up is the same design job as p(Loom)'s eye on the roof.

Shot 2's picture is his second version: the first had a violet and magenta sky that didn't match the in-engine dawn of shots 1, 3 and 4, so he regenerated it as a dusty dawn in the game's own colours. The intro's style block first (with 8:9 and 480 × 540), intro still 5 attached, then the scene:

> Scene: the same flat-topped corporate slab tower from the attached picture, at sunrise just after a storm. The bottom half of the picture is the top of the tower: its upper floors of big plain windows in four columns, the bold zig-zag rug band near the top, and the glass pavilion on the roof. The pavilion is dark and broken, some panes gone, with no glow at all. Loose pale threads hang over the roof edge down the top floors. The windows are mostly dark, with the sunrise reflecting dull orange on their right edges. Behind the roof, a tiny low skyline far below, and the sun just rising at the right, low on the horizon. The top half of the picture is open sky: a muted, dusty dawn with no violet or magenta, in flat horizontal bands from the top down: deep slate indigo (#25224a), dusky plum (#3b2d56), muted mauve (#613c5a), brick rose (#965050), burnt orange (#c87146), amber (#ea9c52), pale gold at the horizon (#fce289). A few torn storm clouds, dark plum with dull orange undersides. Calm, quiet, a little battered. Clear spot: the whole top half of the sky and the air just above the pavilion, empty, for the souls.

Oct 16: the owner had ChatGPT add p(Loom) to it (his earlier picture attached, with a crop of the game's loom at dawn as a reference): standing on the glass pavilion's roof, turned three-quarters toward the sunrise (owner: it should read as rotated from the roof's view of it), dead-eyed, threads snapped, a wisp of smoke, so the shot shows what the souls are leaving. Tries that toppled it beside the pavilion or hung it off the edge were too busy or too small to read.

The source picture stays out of the repo, as with the intro's; `tools/unruggabull-ii/ending/stills.py` makes the game's data from it.
